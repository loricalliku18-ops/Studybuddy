Spaced Repetition: How to Remember Information for the Long Term

Have you ever studied for hours, only to forget most of the information a few days later? You are not alone. Forgetting is a natural part of how the human brain works. However, there is a powerful learning technique that can help you retain information for much longer: Spaced Repetition.

What Is Spaced Repetition?

Spaced repetition is a learning technique that involves reviewing information at gradually increasing intervals instead of studying everything in one session.

How to Use It

  • Day 1: Learn new information.
  • Day 2: Review what you learned.
  • Day 4: Review it again.
  • Day 7: Test your memory.
  • Day 14: Review the material.
  • Day 30: Complete another revision session.

5 Tips

  1. Use Flashcards
  2. Avoid Passive Reading
  3. Focus on Difficult Topics
  4. Be Consistent
  5. Track Your Progress
