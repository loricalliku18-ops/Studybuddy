1. What is StudyBuddy - Teacher Mira?
Free smart study assistant that helps students learn from PDF and PowerPoint with smart keyword search in their own material. Teacher Mira is a virtual character, inspired by Mira Murati, Albanian engineer and former CTO of OpenAI.

2. Your Documents - How they are processed:
Documents are processed by JavaScript code that runs in your browser. The code we wrote for StudyBuddy does not send your document to any server - our code contains no fetch, XMLHttpRequest, sendBeacon, WebSocket, form-submit or external iframe that sends document content. The document is read from your device and stays in your browser memory until you clear it or close the page.
What we do not control: The page loads libraries from external services (cdnjs for PDF.js / JSZip) and - only if you click Accept - the Google Analytics script. These services are independent from us and we cannot guarantee their future behaviour. When your browser requests those files, the respective service sees the normal technical request (IP address, browser type). Those library-file requests do not contain your document, because the document is not part of the request - it is read later, locally, after the library has arrived. You can verify this yourself: DevTools → Network.
Upload limits: Max 15 MB per file, max 150 PDF pages, max 100 PPTX slides, max 2 million characters. For display and analysis, max 5000 sentences are analyzed - if the document has more than 5000 sentences, you will be notified and must split the document to analyze the rest. These limits are to avoid freezing your phone / browser.
